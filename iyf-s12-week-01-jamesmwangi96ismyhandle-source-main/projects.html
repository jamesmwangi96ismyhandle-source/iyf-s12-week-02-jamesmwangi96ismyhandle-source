Projects and Initiatives

These are examples of projects I am interested in developing through teaching, coaching and student mentorship.

1. School Agriculture and Food Production

An initiative to help learners understand practical agriculture, crop production, poultry keeping, soil conservation and sustainable food production through school-based activities.

Skills: Agricultural education, practical learning and planning.

2. Football Coaching and Talent Development

A proposed programme to support learners in developing football skills, teamwork, discipline, fitness and sportsmanship through structured training sessions.

Skills: Coaching, teamwork, leadership and mentorship.

3. Handball Skills Development

A proposed school sports initiative focused on teaching handball fundamentals, improving coordination, building confidence and encouraging fair play.

Skills: Sports instruction, communication and team development.

4. Student Mentorship and Life Skills

An idea for supporting learners through positive guidance, goal setting, teamwork and responsible decision-making in both classroom and sports environments.

Skills: Mentorship, communication and student development.

Future Goals

I aim to connect agricultural education, sports and technology to create engaging learning experiences and opportunities for young people.
